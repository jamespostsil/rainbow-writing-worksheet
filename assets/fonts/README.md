# Fonts

All fonts here may be redistributed, so they are committed and the skill
works on a fresh machine with nothing to download.

## OpenMoji-black-glyf.ttf — the pictures

OpenMoji by HfG Schwäbisch Gmünd, **CC BY-SA 4.0**
(https://openmoji.org, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The *black* variant is used because its glyphs are drawn as single-line art,
which prints in black and white and can be colored in. (Filled emoji fonts
such as Noto Emoji look like silhouettes, and stroking their outlines doubles
every line.)

**Attribution:** worksheets used at home or in a class need nothing extra.
If the sheets are shared publicly or sold, credit "Pictures: OpenMoji, CC
BY-SA 4.0" on the sheet or in the listing.

Source: `openmoji-font.zip` from
https://github.com/hfg-gmuend/openmoji/releases

## Poppins — the letters and all text

Poppins Regular / SemiBold / Bold, SIL Open Font License.
The giant hollow letters are Poppins Bold. `scripts/letterform.py` measures
this exact file, so swapping fonts means re-checking every letter's strokes.
